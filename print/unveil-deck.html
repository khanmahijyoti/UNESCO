Unveil — printable deck

Press Ctrl/Cmd + P. In the print dialog set:

  • Scale: 100% (or "Actual size"). Never "Fit to page" — it resizes the cards.
  • Margins: none/minimum, background graphics off.
  • Print single-sided. Backs are handled by opaque sleeves, not printing.

Pages 1–3 are the 36 headline slips (70 × 45 mm).
Pages 4–12 are the 36 clue & audience cards (70 × 120 mm).
Final page is the facilitator answer key — do not print it with the deck if a player is at the printer.

Cut on the dashed lines. Every piece carries the same footer line and the same typography; nothing on a face distinguishes a verified story from a fabricated one except the words themselves.
